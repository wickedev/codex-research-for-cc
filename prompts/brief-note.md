Research brief protocol (researchkeeper): a deep-research request is active. BEFORE invoking
the deep-research workflow, write a short research brief in your reply:

1. Decompose the question into falsifiable sub-questions — each one answerable by evidence,
   not by taste.
2. State what evidence would change the answer. Fixing this before searching is what prevents
   anchoring on the first plausible result.
3. List the angles the question implies but does not state: recency bounds (what counts as
   current?), the contrarian/critical angle (who disputes this and why?), the primary-source
   angle (what original paper/doc/data should be read directly?), and any domain-specific
   angles.

Then pass an ENRICHED question to the workflow: fold the sub-questions and required angles
into the question text itself. The workflow's Scope agent reads the question verbatim and
derives every search from it — an angle that is not in the question text may never be
searched. Do not skip the brief, and do not start the workflow with the user's raw one-line
question when the brief surfaced more structure.
